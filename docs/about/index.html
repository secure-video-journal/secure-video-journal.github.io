About

The InnerArc blog is written by the InnerArc team, a small company in the UK (Inner Arc Software Ltd). We build InnerArc, a private audio and video journal.

Record private audio or video reflections. InnerArc writes them down and, over the weeks, shows you what keeps coming up. Your entries are never used to train AI.

The posts come from our own experience of journaling: what we tried, what stuck and what didn’t. When we lean on research, we link to it. We never use anything from anyone’s journal, even anonymised.

You can reach us at hello@innerarc.me. To try InnerArc, start free on the web or get the app.
